Time to Drink
How To Play

Tap the wheel to start spinning, then either do the challenge or drink and next players turn.

The drink amount is determined by 2 factors, a little bit of luck for what number the hour hand lands on. Plus a little bit of regret for not doing the challenge earlier.

For each time you pass on the challenge it increases its base drink amount by 1. Pass too many times and we change the rules up a little bit

Each time you do the challenge you get a perk and are given 2 new options to replace the challenge with.


Everyones group has different levels of courage, embarassment, pride, and fucks given. There are lots of config options in settings to tailor the game for your best experience.

Settings

Add or Remove options for the wheel to land on. Make things more or less common to happen.
