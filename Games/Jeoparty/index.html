How To Play

Based on the TV show Jeopardy.

5 Categories are labeled at the top, each having 5 questions in it, simply numbered 1 through 5. Taking turns, a player selects one of the questions and is given 20 seconds to answer it. If they answer correctly in time, they give out X many drinks by the number of the question and go again. Or if they answer wrong or run out of time, they take X many drinks by the number of the question and it is the next players turn.

There are numerous settings to this game from time limit to selecting your categories.

4 Categories will randomly be selected. The 5th Category is always "Random", which contains 5 random questions across all categories in the database.

While not always the case, IN GENERAL, the lower the drink cost, the easier the question.
